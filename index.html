01 / Research

Research interests

Nuclear materials degradation

Corrosion and degradation of structural materials in light water reactor environments.

Microstructure design

Optimizing austenitic stainless steel microstructures to improve stress corrosion cracking (SCC) resistance in high-temperature water.

Cracking mechanisms

Investigating environmentally assisted cracking and mitigation strategies through experimental characterization.

Service life

Developing materials design approaches to extend the operating life of nuclear power plants.
